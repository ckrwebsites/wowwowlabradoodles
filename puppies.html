Additional Info

Occasionally we do sell dogs as breeders. Please Contact us for Breeder Agreements and Pricing. 

We sometimes have a puppy available for a Guardian Home. Guardian Homes allow us diversity in our breeding program, and offer our breeder-quality puppies the opportunity to live a pampered life as a family pet and companion. For more information, check out our Guardian Program.

Please don't forget to thoroughly read our Puppy Pet Contract so that you are fully informed of the terms and conditions involved in purchasing and owning a WOW WOW Australian Labradoodle. 

We use a safe and dependable airline service out of Houston. The purchaser pays for all shipping charges (approximately $700, which includes air fare, crate, health certificate, and flight insurance). We will provide a health certificate and flight insurance, and we prefer to ship on direct flights if possible. The full purchase price and shipping charges must be paid before we send your puppy.
