Purchase Policy

We welcome you to visit our home to select your puppy. However, if you are unable to visit, we will work with you to select the puppy of your choice. A nonrefundable deposit of $500 will hold your puppy until it is ready for its new home, with the balance due before the puppy leaves our home.  

Most of our puppies are sold as pets with a pet contract that states the new owner will spay or neuter the dog before it is 12 months of age. Occasionally we do sell dogs as breeders - please inquire for more information.

Health Guarantee

We guarantee that the puppy we provide to you will be healthy. Please take your puppy to the vet of your choice within one week of purchase, and if there should be any health issues, we will gladly replace your puppy or refund your money. In addition, the puppy comes with a two year health guarantee which covers any serious genetic health issues.

Shipping

We use a safe and dependable airline service out of Houston. The purchaser pays for all shipping charges (approximately $700, which includes air fare, crate, health certificate, and flight insurance). We will provide a health certificate and flight insurance, and we prefer to ship on direct flights if possible. The full purchase price and shipping charges must be paid before we send your puppy.
